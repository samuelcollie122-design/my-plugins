hrtf_kemar.bin holds SpaceTrack's measured head responses for the 3D sound.

Source: MIT KEMAR dummy-head measurements, normal pinna, 710 directions, in the SOFA version from
https://sofacoustics.org/data/database/mit/mit_kemar_normal_pinna.sofa

Credit: W. G. Gardner and K. D. Martin, "HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR Dummy-Head Microphone",
MIT Media Lab, 1994. Copyright 1994 MIT Media Laboratory. Provided free of use restrictions,
provided the authors are cited when the data is used in research or commercial work.
https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html

The file is rebuilt with Tools/convert_hrtf.py, which also describes its format and processing.
